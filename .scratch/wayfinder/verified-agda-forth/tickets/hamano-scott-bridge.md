## Question

Which concrete Hamano–Scott-style polarized categorical model is appropriate for interpreting the project's MALLP derivations and relating their resource discipline to compiler core and target stacks?

Study the reflective/coreflective polarized subcategories, the ambient *-autonomous structure, products/coproducts, polarity shifts, and the paper's limits on MALLP full completeness. Compare a free syntactic model with a concrete category of resource/stack interpretations.

## Required result

Define the interpretation of each MALLP formula and inference rule as an object/morphism, then state exactly what soundness and cut-elimination invariance provide. Construct a separate, explicit adequacy/usage bridge to the source core and typed Forth stack AST. Do not treat categorical soundness as an operational compiler-correctness theorem, and do not claim full completeness.
