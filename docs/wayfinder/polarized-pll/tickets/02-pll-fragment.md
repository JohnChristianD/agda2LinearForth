# Decision ticket: Define the exact polarized linear logic fragment

Type: research

## Question

What is the smallest fragment of Olivier Laurent's polarized linear logic that can be represented and normalized in Agda and executed through the existing Forth backend without overstating the result?

## Required evidence

- Specify positive/negative formulas and their constructors.
- Specify typing judgments and the exact linear context discipline.
- Decide whether the first milestone includes additives, multiplicatives, exponentials, second order, control operators, or cut elimination. Do not silently claim omitted rules.
- Define proof terms or proof nets, reduction/cut elimination, and the property to prove (preservation, progress, normalization, or a stated subset).
- Compare the chosen fragment against Laurent's polarized proof-net presentation and explain exclusions.

## Resolution format

A closed grammar, typing rules, operational semantics, and theorem statement that can be encoded without new classical assumptions. No implementation in this ticket.
